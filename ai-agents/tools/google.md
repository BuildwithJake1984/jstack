# Google — Gemini and Google AI

## When to use

- Building with **Gemini** via AI Studio, the Gemini API, or Google Cloud AI products.
- Comparing Google's multimodal and long-context offerings with other frontier labs.
- Tracking Google AI blog and DeepMind research that affects product choices.

## What it is

[Google AI](https://ai.google/) is Google's umbrella for AI products and research. **Gemini** is the consumer and developer-facing model family ([gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com/)); builders use [Google AI for Developers](https://ai.google.dev/) for APIs, SDKs, and documentation. Research and model announcements often flow through **Google DeepMind**.

## Where it fits in Jake's stack and JStack

Google is a **frontier lab option** when Gemini fits pricing, modality, or ecosystem needs (Google Cloud, Workspace, or mobile).
