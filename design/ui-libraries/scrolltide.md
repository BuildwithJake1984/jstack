# Scrolltide — cinematic scroll templates and AI prompts

## When to use

- Shipping marketing or product sites that should feel scroll-driven and cinematic rather than generic AI layout.
- Dropping a single high-polish section, interaction, or background into an existing page without rebuilding the whole site.
- Briefing Cursor, Claude Code, Codex, v0, or similar tools with a curated prompt instead of inventing motion from scratch.

Listed in [UI Components catalog](components.md).

[Scrolltide](https://www.scrolltide.co/) is a curated library of scroll-driven website **templates** and **prompts**. The site positions it as: copy a template or prompt, paste it into your AI tool, and launch a site that does not look AI-made. The library also sells **full source code** on paid plans.

## What it offers

At review time the homepage highlighted **127 templates** in the library and **10,000+ builders**. Content is grouped roughly as:

- **Full templates** — landing pages, hero sections, and 3D website examples (named showcases such as VALMORA, Synapse, Vesper).
- **One piece at a time** — individual prompts for buttons, carousels, scroll-linked effects, 3D pieces, and similar UI fragments you can drop into work already in progress.
- **Motion backgrounds** — GPU-oriented animated backgrounds (gradients, aurora, liquid metal, fire, mesh flows); export as **MP4**, stills, or components for heroes.
- **Sections** — reusable blocks such as pricing tables, testimonial walls, footers, forms, and bento-style layouts (**19 sections** called out on the homepage).
- **UI screens** — dashboards, wallets, and consoles for post-signup product UI (**14 UI screens**), filterable by categories such as commerce, finance, health, and property.

New templates are advertised **every week** (pricing page: new prompts every Friday).

## Use notes

- Treat Scrolltide as a **taste-led template and prompt library**, not a unified design system. Adapt output to project tokens, component inventory, and accessibility (including reduced motion).
- Pair with [**shadcn/ui**](shadcn.md) or the project's existing component foundation for app chrome; Scrolltide targets high-motion marketing and section-level drops.
- **Reference only** — not a registered agent skill. Review copied source, dependencies, performance, and licensing before shipping client work.
