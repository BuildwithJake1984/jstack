# Kage — real-product interface inspiration and agent prompts

## When to use

- Use when researching a specific section—navigation, hero, feature grid, pricing, testimonials, calls to action, forms, footers, cards, charts, and more—or when matching a design direction such as minimal, editorial, playful, glass, monochrome, or bento.

Listed in the [UI Components catalog](components.md).

A searchable inspiration library built from real product interfaces. It organizes complete designs and more than 1,500 captured components by function, style, industry, color, and technology stack, then provides prompts for Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor.

## Access and licensing

**Free to browse and use as inspiration.** Kage states that screenshots belong to their respective products and are presented for inspiration and commentary. Treat the prompts as starting points and create original executions.


## Cautions

- Do not reproduce captured third-party interfaces pixel for pixel.
- Validate prompt output against the project's approved design system and project requirements.
- Source screenshots demonstrate visible presentation, not hidden logic, accessibility, responsive states, or performance.
- Kage's separate tooling directory includes third-party paid and free products; each retains its own pricing and license.
